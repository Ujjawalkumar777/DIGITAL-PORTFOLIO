About The Game

Prince of Persia: The Forgotten Sands is an action-adventure game developed by Ubisoft Montreal and published by Ubisoft. It was released in 2010 as a part of the Prince of Persia series.The Storyline is set between Prince of Persia:The sand of Time and warrior within. In this chapter, the Prince embarks on a perilous journey to save his brother Malik's kingdom from a powerful ancient army. The game combines classic acrobatic gameplay with brand-new elemental powers, allowing you to freeze water into solid ice and manipulate time itself.

Key Features:

⚔️ Classic Combat
Experience the energetic, fast-paced hack-and-slash combat against massive hordes of enemies.

🏃‍♂️ Amazing Parkour
Scale epic castle walls, swing from pillars, and navigate through deadly environmental traps.

💧 Elemental Powers
Master the elements of nature—Water, Earth, Wind, and Fire—to solve complex puzzles and defeat bosses.
